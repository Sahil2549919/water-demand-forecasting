01 — Problem

Why Water Demand Forecasting Matters

Water is one of our most valuable resources, yet predicting future demand remains a major challenge for cities and municipalities.

📈
+18–30%

Changing Demand

Seasonal weather shifts and heatwaves create fast, difficult-to-predict demand swings.

🏙
+2.4% / year

Growing Population

Urban expansion increases household and commercial water load beyond simple averages.

⚠
14–22% loss

Inefficient Planning

Old planning models overlook real usage spikes and leak-driven losses in distribution.

💧
Supply risk

Resource Wastage

Without reliable forecasting, cities overproduce, underprepare, and face service disruption.

Accurate forecasting enables smarter planning, efficient resource utilization, and sustainable water management.

05 — Intelligence

How Our AI Forecasting System Works

Our system follows an intelligent, data-driven process — studying historical patterns to generate reliable predictions for future water demand.

1

Observe Historical Data

The AI analyzes consumption records from previous months and years.

2

Discover Hidden Patterns

Identifies recurring trends and seasonal variations difficult to spot manually.

3

Learn Demand Trends

The system understands how water demand evolves over time.

4

Predict Future Demand

The AI estimates future water demand for upcoming months.

5

Support Better Decisions

Predictions help authorities optimize production, distribution, and reduce wastage.

Key Insight: The AI does not guess the future — it learns from the past to make informed predictions.

Values are million litres per day (MLD). The holdout window is the last 12 months of history; metrics compare predicted vs. actual on that window before projecting the future.

How to read this graph

1

Blue line: historical demand from previous months.

2

Teal dashed line: the forecast for upcoming months.

3

Trend: the model studies long-term growth or decline in demand.

4

Seasonality: demand rises or falls depending on climate and usage patterns.
